# Proposal

## Why

The bot has a verified foundation but no typed operator contract or statistics engine. Group the cohesive offline policy and reporting domain from DEG-4, DEG-6, DEG-14, DEG-21 through DEG-25 and DEG-27 through DEG-31/33/34 before connecting voice or speech adapters.

## What Changes

- Add protected JSON schema, secret-safe preflight/doctor and documented policy defaults.
- Add deterministic Head Hunt affinity/draw and Fixed Channel assignment validation.
- Add encoded built-in/custom lexicon, idempotent minimal detections, actual monitored exposure intervals and SQLite migrations/deletion/backup.
- Add timezone calendar windows, total/per-word/scaled analytics, Discord-sized rendering, independent cadence scheduling, durable outbox/catch-up and overlap-safe retention.
- Provide privacy/opt-out/disable contract and local CLI. No live connection, command adapter, transcription or native installation is claimed in this change.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `operator-configuration`: Typed redacted local configuration, preflight and privacy controls.
- `monitoring-policy`: Eligible-channel affinity and mutually exclusive assignments.
- `speech-statistics`: Minimal idempotent tracked-word events and monitored exposure.
- `calendar-reporting`: Window-scoped rankings, delivery outbox, catch-up and retention.

### Modified Capabilities

None.

## Impact

DisCuss domain/CLI, SQLite state and operator documentation. DiscordVoiceReception remains independent. SDK/runtime integration and installers follow separately; no production server access.
